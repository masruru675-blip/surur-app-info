Delete your Surūr account

You can request deletion from inside Surūr while signed in.

  1. Open Surūr and sign in.
  2. Go to More → Delete account.
  3. Read the explanation, type DELETE, then tap Request account deletion. You need an internet connection.
  4. The app shows your pending request. You can cancel it there before deletion is completed.

What happens next

Your request is recorded for the Surūr operator to process. Sending a request does not immediately remove your account or data. The operator must complete deletion on the server, including handling classroom ownership and shared records without exposing or deleting another person’s data. Your account and associated personal data will be removed when processing is complete, except information that must be retained under applicable law. Contact support.surur@gmail.com if you need an update.

Before you request deletion

Use More → Export your data if you want a copy of your information. Check More → Saved on this device for records still waiting on your device to upload. Deletion is permanent once completed.

Can’t access your account?

Email support.surur@gmail.com for help regaining access or to discuss a privacy request. For a signed-in account, use the in-app deletion option above.

For details about the data involved, read the privacy policy.
