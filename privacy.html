Surūr privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026 · Operator: Masrur · Contact: support.suruًr@gmail.com

Surūr helps people record Qur’an memorisation, revision and recitation, set goals and, if they choose, connect with partners, teachers or classrooms. This policy explains the information used to provide those features.

Information you provide and create

When you make an account, Surūr uses your email address and sign-in credentials through its authentication provider. You may add a username and display name. Surūr stores your onboarding choices, settings, selected muṣḥaf layout, practice and recitation history (including selected āyāt, dates, durations, strengths and any notes you enter), goals, human assessments, notifications, and account deletion requests. If you use connections or classrooms, it also stores invitations, roles, announcements, groups, assignments, attendance and related records. When you contact support, we receive the information you send.

How the information is used

We use this information to sign you in, keep your practice record, calculate progress and revision suggestions, sync records, provide optional partner and classroom features, answer support requests, and handle privacy and deletion requests. Surūr uses Supabase for account authentication and hosted app data. Necessary service and security logs may be processed by the hosting provider.

What other people can see

Your private practice history, notes, settings and goal details are not shown to partners or teachers. A connection may see authorised summary progress. In a classroom, authorised staff can see appropriate student summaries and classroom records such as assignments and attendance. Classroom students see their own assignments, attendance and progress. Invitations and access depend on current account and classroom permissions.

Information on your device

Surūr keeps account-specific copies of recently used information and work awaiting sync so some features continue offline. On supported native devices, private snapshots and pending observations are encrypted using a key held by the device keystore. Sign-out clears that account’s private local cache and pending queue. Public Qur’an reference information and any text you explicitly download may remain on the device. A data export you save or share is under your control; remove copies from other apps or locations yourself.

Reminders and other services

Optional daily check-ins are stored and displayed on your device; this version does not use push notifications. In-app notifications about connections and assessments may be stored with your account. The reviewed version does not use advertising or analytics SDKs. The Qur’an text comes from a verified Tanzil-backed source; the text itself is public reference content.

Retention, export and deletion

Account and practice records are kept while your account is active and as needed to operate Surūr. You can create a CSV export in More → Export your data. To request account deletion, use More → Delete account and follow the steps on the deletion help page. A request is recorded but is not an immediate deletion. The operator processes deletion of your account and associated personal data, while handling shared classroom records and any information that must be retained under applicable law. If you need an update or wish to ask about access, correction or deletion, contact us.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, email support.surur@gmail.com. If the app or its data practices change, this policy will be updated with a new date.
