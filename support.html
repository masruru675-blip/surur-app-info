Surūr support

Need help with your account, a practice record, syncing or a classroom? Contact the person operating Surūr.

Email: support.surur@gmail.com

Operator: Masrur

What to include

Tell us what you were doing, what happened, your device type and the app version if you know it. You can include your account email so we can identify your account. Please do not send your password or private Qur’an notes.

Sync and offline use

Some personal records can be saved on your device while offline and uploaded later. Open More → Saved on this device to check work still waiting to upload. Signing out removes that account’s private local cache and pending work, so check sync status before signing out.
